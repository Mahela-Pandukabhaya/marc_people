Senith Jayakody is an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate with First Class Honours from the University of Peradeniya, Sri Lanka. His research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, signal processing, computational imaging, and applied mathematics. His research interests include biomedical signal processing, multispectral imaging, implicit neural representations, interpretable machine learning, and the mathematical and theoretical foundations of generative models, particularly diffusion models.

At MARC, his research encompasses preterm birth prediction through electrohysterography (EHG) signal decomposition and machine learning, the development of multispectral imaging systems for material characterisation and environmental monitoring, and image colourisation using implicit neural representations. His research on diffusion models focuses on understanding the underlying mathematical and statistical properties of the diffusion process and developing theoretically motivated approaches to improve generative modelling.
